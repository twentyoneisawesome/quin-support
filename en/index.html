Offline-first personal finance — your data lives on your device, with cloud backup to iCloud and Google Drive.

About Landy Money

Landy Money is a personal finance app that runs entirely on your device. There's no sign-up and no login — just open the app and start tracking your accounts, transactions, budgets, and investments. Your financial data is stored locally and is never tied to an online account.

Frequently asked questions

Where is my data stored?

All of your financial data is stored locally in a database on your device. Landy Money is offline-first and requires no account, so your records aren't tied to any login.

Do I need an account or to sign in?

No. Landy Money has no accounts and no login. Everything works the moment you open the app.

Can I back up or move my data to another device?

Yes. Landy Money supports cloud backup to iCloud (iPhone) and Google Drive (Android). In Settings → Data, tap Back up to iCloud or Back up to Google Drive. On your new device, sign in to the same Apple ID or Google account, install Landy Money, and tap Restore from iCloud / Restore from Google Drive to bring everything back. You can also use Export transactions (CSV) to open your transactions in Excel or Numbers.

Does Landy Money connect to the internet?

Core features work fully offline. Some optional features do use the internet: live exchange rates, Taiwan (TWSE) stock prices, e-invoice sync (電子發票, a premium feature in Taiwan), and subscription management. See our Privacy Policy for details.
